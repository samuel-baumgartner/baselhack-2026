The light has restrained natural spectral dispersion: hints of cyan, pale gold, coral and violet only at fine edges. Transparent bulk, visible thickness, polished sharp facets, credible internal refraction and reflection, delicate luminous caustics beneath. The object must feel like real heavy optical crystal, not white paper, opaque plastic or metal. A sculptural piece of precision optics.

Composition/framing: one strong hero object, asymmetrical right-weighted composition, generous whitespace. The prism and split light are the sole focal points.
Lighting/mood: bright studio daylight with a controlled directional beam, subtle grounded shadows, beautiful glass visibility against a light background. No dark setting.
Constraints: no text, letters, numbers, logo or watermark. No paper, cardboard, ivory slabs, UI panels, browser windows, floating dashboard, gradient blobs or decorative glass orbs. Do not render a complete webpage. Use actual glass refraction and caustics rather than a generic rainbow overlay.
